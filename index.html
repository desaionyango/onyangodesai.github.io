Who am I?

My name is Onyango Desai, a student of Riragia Technical and Vocational college from September 2023 to November 2025, having completed my final exams for certificate level in Information and Communication Technology. The core units of study included; perform computer repair and maintenance, perform computer networking, digital literacy, manage database systems, manage operating systems, install computer software, and develop computer program.
The desire to know how websites work, how applications are developed, and the communication between two or more computer devices, drove me to pick ICT as my favorite course. Self-paced learning has also played an important role in shaping this journey, Youtube being my major learning platform. I know and believe that every technical problem can be solved, and AI is here to help human beings accomplish certain tasks in the shortest time possible.

Over the past three years, I have had the opportunity to work as a field enumerator for SATREPS and JKUATES, both non-profit organzations. SATREPS (Science and Technology Research Partnership for Sustainable development) Malaria project is multi-institution multi-disciplinary project involving JICA, the county government of Homa bay, Mount Kenya University, Otaro University, and Nagasaki University with the aim of eliminating malaria within the lake regions Homa bay county, and Kenya as a whole. The community was regarded an important partner in this study. I worked as a field enumerator on a contract from August 2021 to May 2025, in Suba south constituency. In this role, I helped mobilize community members by explaining the major aim of the study, conducted interviews and collected data based on the participants' responses, ensuring data accuracy before submission, increased participants' awareness on the various practices and ways to prevent the spread of malaria. The main survey applications used for field data collection were RedCap and Census Survey Entry.
JKUATES (Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology Enterprise) aim was to study the Effect of Drone Technology in Improving Health care outcomes in Homa bay county. This was a two-day contract, 16th and 17th of April, 2025. I was trained on questionnaire administration on maternal and child health care, and the actual data collection using KoBoCollect survey application.

In addition, I undergone three months period of attachment with KE0263 Compassion International Kenya- Lwanda CDC Gwassi, from January to April, 2025. This organisation partners with the church to lift children and youth out of economic, physical, spiritual and social poverty. Over the attachment period, I assisted in tutoring children, youth, and select-parents on Computer packages, conducted school and home visits on the welfare of children and caregivers, assisted in data entry and organisation, and basic analysis(PDF payments) using Microsoft Word and Excel, assisted in maintaining office cleanliness, and ensured professionalism throughout.

Every oragnisation, every individual I interacted with, helped in shaping my skills in communication, teamwork, time management, data handling, attention to detail, and problem solving.
